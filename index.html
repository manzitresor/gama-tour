Who we are

A Rwandan team planning journeys across East Africa, from home to anywhere.

Gama Travel & Tour Agency is based in Kigali and works across Rwanda and its neighbours. The name Gama stands for strength, unity and movement. The team's approach is community-first: journeys rooted in local knowledge, cultural immersion and responsible tourism.

Every Gama journey is planned to support local communities and to help protect the natural and cultural heritage that travellers come to see.

Where we travel

Rainforest, volcanoes, savannah and lake, all within a day of Kigali.

Rwanda is small enough to see a great deal of it in one trip. When you're ready to go further, Gama's cross-border itineraries continue into the rest of East Africa.

  • Volcanoes National Park North · ≈2.5 h Mountain gorillas, golden monkeys and the Virunga volcano chain.
  • Nyungwe Forest Southwest · ≈5 h Chimpanzees, 300+ bird species and a canopy walkway 60 m above the forest floor.
  • Akagera National Park East · ≈2.5 h Rwanda's savannah park, and its Big Five country.
  • Lake Kivu West · ≈3 h Island boat trips, hot springs and long lakeside sunsets.
  • Burera & Ruhondo North · ≈2.5 h Twin lakes, grey crowned cranes and volcano views.
  • Kigali Centre · start here Memorials, museums, eco-parks and the city after dark.
  • Beyond Rwanda Uganda · Kenya · Tanzania · Burundi Cross-border itineraries, including gorilla trekking in Bwindi.
